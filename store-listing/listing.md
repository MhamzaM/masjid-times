## Full description

Jamaat times of the masjids around you, kept up to date by the people who pray there. Free, with no ads and no account.

Prayer-time apps calculate when a prayer *starts*. Masjid Times shows when the **jamaat** actually begins at each masjid, so you can catch it at the one nearest to you, whether you're at home, at work or travelling across town.

• Nearby masjids on a map or in a list sorted by distance, with search
• A timing board for your masjid with the next jamaat and a countdown
• Reminders 5–30 minutes before each jamaat, set per prayer
• Jumuah time on Fridays
• Alerts when a jamaat time changes at a masjid you follow
• Maghrib as "sunset + 5 minutes", updated every day by itself
• Add a masjid that's missing from the map
• English, اردو and پښتو

HOW TIMES STAY ACCURATE
Nobody can change a time on their own. A new time is a proposal, and it goes live only when other people standing at that masjid agree. Phones using fake-GPS apps are refused, impossible times (such as Fajr after sunrise) are rejected, and people whose reports turn out correct gain more weight over time. Every time shows how many people confirmed it and when.

PRIVACY
No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your exact location is never stored, only how far you were from the masjid when you contributed.

Part of FreeApps by Hamza: free apps, supported by donations.

## Data safety answers

- **Data collected:** Approximate and precise location. Purpose: app functionality and fraud prevention. Not shared, and processed only in the moment; only the distance to the masjid is kept. The user can't turn it off for contributing, but browsing works without it.
- **App activity / other user-generated content:** reported jamaat times and masjid names. Purpose: app functionality.
- **Device or other IDs:** a random anonymous account ID. Purpose: fraud prevention and app functionality.
- **Data shared with third parties:** none.
- **Encrypted in transit:** yes (HTTPS).
- **Users can request deletion:** yes, via the GitHub issues link in the privacy policy.

## Permissions declaration

- **SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM:** jamaat reminders must fire at a specific minute. The app falls back to inexact alarms if the user doesn't allow it.
- **Location (foreground only):** to find nearby masjids and to verify that a contributor is at the masjid.
